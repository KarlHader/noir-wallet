title: Shielded funds
description: Receive and move funds with Zcash Shield and NEAR Confidential.
icon: ShieldCheck
---

Select **Shield** on Home, then open **Providers**. The available providers depend on the account and network.

## Zcash Shield

Use this provider for native shielded ZEC. Select **Shield** to move transparent ZEC into the Zcash shielded pool, or **Unshield** to move shielded ZEC to a transparent address. Review the amount and fee, then follow the transaction in **Activity**.

To receive shielded ZEC, choose **Receive > ZEC > Zcash Shield**. Confirm that the sender supports shielded Zcash addresses.

<Callout type="info">
  Shielding leaves the original transparent source visible on-chain. Transfers between shielded
  addresses protect the addresses and amount on-chain.
</Callout>

## NEAR Confidential

Use this provider for eligible assets on mainnet. To fund from Public, select **Shield**, choose the asset and **NEAR Confidential**, and review the quote. To receive funds externally, choose **Receive**, then select the token, network, and provider before copying the deposit address.

Use the exact token and network shown for an external deposit. To move funds back to a public address, select **Unshield** and verify the destination network, amount, and fee. Follow settlement in **Activity**.
