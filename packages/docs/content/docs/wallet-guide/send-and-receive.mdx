## Receive ZEC

Open **Receive** and choose the address appropriate for the sender.

| Address     | On-chain privacy                                  | Use when                                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Shielded    | Amount and address details are protected by Zcash | The sender supports shielded Zcash addresses |
| Transparent | Transaction details are public                    | Compatibility requires a transparent address |

Copy the full address or let the sender scan the QR code. Verify the beginning and end of the address after pasting.

To reuse an earlier shielded address, open the active account's **Zcash settings** and choose
**Shielded addresses**. Select an address to make it the default receiving address; **Current**
marks the selected address. Copying an address does not change the selection.

## Send ZEC

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the destination

    Paste the recipient address in **Send To** and confirm that the address is on the intended network.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the funding source

    Open the **Shielded** or **Transparent** control beside **Amount** to choose the source.
    The selector shows each source's available and pending funds. Transparent funding can reveal
    and link the selected public UTXOs; it is unavailable for Keystone accounts.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the amount

    On Zcash, Available shows funds you can use from the selected source before this transaction's network fee. Pending funds cannot be used yet. Enter a destination before selecting **Max**; its estimate accounts for transaction selection, fees, and dust constraints.

    Received Zcash funds meet the wallet's confirmation requirement after 3 confirmations. They appear as available once synchronized and eligible for selection. In **Activity**, an external incoming transaction can still show **Confirming** until it reaches 10 confirmations, even when its funds are available. If you do not trust the sender, wait for more confirmations before spending them.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review and approve

    Check the destination, amount, funding source, memo, and fee before broadcasting.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send EVM assets

Choose a **Public** or **Confidential** source. Confidential sends use the privacy route for supported assets; unsupported assets are sent directly on-chain from the same address. Review any privacy warning before choosing **Send anyway**. If availability cannot be checked, choose **Retry**.

After a direct EVM transfer is submitted, choose **Done** to return to the wallet or **View on Explorer** to open the configured block explorer. The transfer remains in **Activity** for status and recovery.

## Private memos

Zcash shielded transactions can include a private memo. The memo field is disabled for a transparent recipient. Treat a memo as durable transaction data: only include information you intend the recipient to keep.

<Callout type="warn">
  A transparent transaction exposes public on-chain details. Sending from transparent funds to a
  shielded address does not hide the transparent source.
</Callout>
