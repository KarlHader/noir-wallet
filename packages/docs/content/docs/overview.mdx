Noir Wallet is a self-custodial Chrome wallet for Zcash and supported EVM networks. It encrypts wallet credentials on your device.

## What you can do

- Send and receive ZEC and supported EVM assets.
- Use native Zcash shielding or NEAR Confidential for eligible assets.
- Swap supported assets, access lending on mainnet, and connect to supported dApps.
- Connect [Ledger or Keystone](/wallet-guide/install-and-setup#hardware-wallets) for supported operations.

<Callout type="warn" title="Self-custody means responsibility">
  Noir Wallet cannot recover your seed phrase. Store it offline and never share it with a website,
  support account, or person.
</Callout>
